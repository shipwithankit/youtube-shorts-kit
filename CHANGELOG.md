# Changelog

## 2.0.0 – 2026-11-19

First public release.

- ⚡ One-click panel on any Short with **Overview, Tags, Transcript and ✨ Rewrite** tabs
- Transcript in the original language or **English** (YouTube translation), timestamps, `.txt` / `.srt` export
- YouTube Studio **tags** with one-click copy and a 500-character counter
- **Views/day** and an **outlier score** vs the channel's typical Short
- **Hook** (first 3 seconds) highlighted
- **AI rewrite** with Google Gemini: full script, 5 hooks, or titles + tags, in your own style
- **Library** with search, filters, sorting, CSV/Excel export and JSON backup
- **Tag insights** across saved Shorts
- **Bulk grab**: tags and stats for a whole channel's Shorts
- Keyboard shortcuts: Alt+T analyze, Alt+S save, Esc close
